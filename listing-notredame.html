About This Stay

Notre-Dame Cathedral invites guests into the dramatic heart of Parisian Gothic architecture. Built beginning in 1163 (and roughly speaking, finished by 1260), its combination of soaring nave, sculptural portals, and radiating rose windows exemplifies Gothic's love for ornament and light. Guests often remark on the cathedral's ability to make everyone feel just a little more illuminated.
