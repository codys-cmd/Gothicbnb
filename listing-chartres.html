About This Stay

Chartres was a pilgrimage powerhouse, drawing travelers seeking healing or wisdom. Constructed between 1194 and 1220, the building features an almost innumerous number of things to see. From beautiful stained-glass windows, distinctive twin spires, or local celebreties hanging about the royal portal, you'll find yourself wandering in circles seeing everything this destination has to offer (and literally so, if you find yourself wandering the labyrinth).
