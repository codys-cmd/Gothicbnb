About This Stay

Salisbury Cathedral offers a serene countryside escape wrapped in some of the most beautiful views you'll ever encounter. Built between 1220 and 1330, the church provides a literally rock-solid foundation to the Gothic style in England. The famous 404-foot spire, added in the 14th century, is the tallest church spire in the United Kingdom.
